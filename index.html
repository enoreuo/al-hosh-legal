المحادثات الفردية (واحد-إلى-واحد) مشفَّرة من طرف إلى طرف (End-to-End Encryption)، بحيث لا يستطيع أحد قراءتها سواك ومن تحادثه — ولا حتى نحن. مفاتيحك الخاصة محفوظة على جهازك، مع نسخة احتياطية مشفَّرة تُمكِّنك من استرجاع محادثاتك عند تغيير الجهاز.

Al-Hosh — Privacy Policy

At Al-Hosh we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1) Data we collect

  • Email address — to create and verify your account via a one-time code.
  • Phone number — optional, to discover contacts who use the app.
  • Name & profile photo — to display your identity in chats.
  • Contacts — with your permission, to suggest friends already on Al-Hosh.
  • Message & media content — to deliver your messages to their recipients.
  • Location — used only in the "Nafeer" mutual-aid feature to alert nearby people, and only with your permission.

Contacts sync (important): When you choose to find friends already on Al-Hosh, the app shows a clear in-app dialog asking for your consent once before any sync begins. Only after you agree, the phone numbers from your contacts are sent to our server solely to match them against registered Al-Hosh users. We do not store these numbers on our servers, and we never share or sell them. If you decline, you can keep using the rest of the app.

2) Message encryption

One-to-one chats are end-to-end encrypted, so no one but you and the person you're chatting with can read them — not even us. Your private keys are stored on your device, with an encrypted backup that lets you restore your chats when you change devices.

3) Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on our own servers. We do not sell your data or share it with any advertiser or third party.

4) Advertising

Al-Hosh contains no ads and uses no advertising trackers.

5) Deleting your data

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing our support address. We will delete your data within a reasonable period after verifying your identity.

6) Data security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. Please keep your verification code confidential and never share it.

7) Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will appear in the app and on this page.

9) Contact

1) Strictly prohibited

It is absolutely forbidden to use Al-Hosh to:

  • Share any content that sexualises a minor, whether real, edited, drawn, or AI-generated.
  • Groom a child, or contact a child for any sexual purpose.
  • Commit sextortion, or threaten to publish a minor's private images.
  • Traffic children, facilitate trafficking, or arrange to meet a child for a sexual purpose.
  • Promote, normalise, or defend these acts, or share links or means of accessing them.

2) How to report a violation

If you see this kind of content or behaviour on Al-Hosh, report it to us immediately by email: alhush621@gmail.com

Please include whatever you can:

  • The username, phone number, or email of the reported account.
  • A description of what happened, with the approximate date and time.
  • Screenshots if you have them. Do not send us child sexual abuse material — describe it instead, as possessing or forwarding it is a crime.

We treat child safety reports as our highest priority and aim to respond within 48 hours.

3) Child safety point of contact

The designated contact for child safety matters at Al-Hosh is: alhoshchildsafty@gmail.com

  • We remove the violating content.
  • We permanently terminate the offending account and bar its holder from returning.
  • We preserve the evidence available to us for the competent authorities.
  • We refer the case to the competent authorities where applicable law requires it.

5) Minimum age

Al-Hosh is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under that age, we close the account and delete its data.

6) A note on encryption and the scope of review

One-to-one chats on Al-Hosh are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read them or scan them automatically on our servers. Anything reported to us, along with content in channels, groups, and bots, can be reviewed and acted upon.
